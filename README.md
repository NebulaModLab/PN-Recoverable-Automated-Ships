## What does this mod do?
This mod allows the recovery of automated ships without requiring the Automated Ships skill. 
Recovery happens same as vanilla post-battle, however you can also recover derelict automated ships
which does have a LunaSetting to disable the feature in case of UI/dialog bugs.

**NOTE:** This does not allow you to field them in combat as well, for that you still need the skill.

## Why was this made?
I've seen a multitude of people wonder why we cannot recover automated ships before being able to field them in combat.
They feel/felt like you should be able to recover them after fighting them in combat as to not lose out on obtaining any.
And since I saw that as a good enough reason to make it happen, I decided to make this small mod!
